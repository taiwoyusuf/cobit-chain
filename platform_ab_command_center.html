AI outputs must be cleared before they enter consequential workflows.

Platform A now evaluates whether an AI-generated output can safely enter the intended workflow, regulated workflow, decision-support pathway, release decision, or escalation path. Output clearance binds intended use, risk, lineage, explainability, human approval, evidence, and final clearance decision.
Engineering position: an AI output is not operationally trusted because it was generated. It becomes trusted only when context, lineage, risk, evidence, human approval, and clearance conditions are satisfied.
