# Platform B v1 Release Summary

## Release status

Platform B v1 is frozen as of 2026-07-03.

No new lifecycle stages, modules, pillars, dashboards, architectures, or foundational concepts should be added to v1.

## Release position

Platform B v1 is an Operational Trust Infrastructure for AI-enabled regulated, cyber-physical, agentic, evidence-sensitive, and human-critical environments.

It continuously demonstrates whether AI-enabled systems, workflows, agents, evidence, outputs, and actions remain trustworthy in operation.

## Locked lifecycle

Discovery -> Visibility -> Governance -> Operationalization -> Manufacturing Monitoring -> Evidence -> Continuous Assurance -> Operational Trust

## Major capability themes

- AI operational trust assurance
- AI cyber assurance
- Runtime authority assurance
- Reachable consequence assurance
- Human oversight effectiveness assurance
- Regulatory evidence assurance
- Regulatory confidence and defensibility assurance
- AI RMF-to-assurance evidence bridge
- Enterprise control-layer assurance
- Context-assured wearable and cyber-physical assurance
- Physical state evidence assurance
- Cross-framework incident taxonomy assurance
- Pre-deviation and CAPA effectiveness assurance
- AI-designed asset provenance assurance

## Intentionally excluded from v1

Platform B v1 does not replace QMS, CAPA, deviation management, change control, validation systems, cybersecurity platforms, IAM/PAM systems, ServiceNow, workflow systems, or regulatory systems of record.

New ideas must be captured in the backlog as v1.1, v1.2, or v2 candidates.
