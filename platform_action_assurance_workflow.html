Platform A / Action Assurance / Operational Action Governance

A chatbot produces information. An AI agent performs actions.

Platform A therefore governs operational actions, not only AI outputs. The Action Assurance Workflow is a reusable capability across Agent Passport, Tool Call Evidence Ledger, Operational Trust, Evidence Vault, AI Output Clearance, Decision Confidence, and Knowledge Integrity.
Engineering principle: cloud platforms execute AI agents. COBIT-Chain evaluates whether AI-assisted actions are operationally trustworthy before they affect regulated systems. Every action must pass through Intent, Authority, Context, Policy and Risk Evaluation, Human Approval where required, Execution, State Verification, Evidence Recording, and Continuous Assurance.
