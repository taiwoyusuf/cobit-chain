The live trust object behind every assurance decision.

Operational Trust Twins represent the real-world object being governed: equipment, CI, workflow, AI agent, wearable interface, or platform service. Each twin carries operational state, evidence package, dependencies, trust score, active gaps, confidence, and next action.
Twin principle: COBIT-Chain should not only show dashboards. It should maintain a live trust object for each governed asset or workflow. The twin becomes the bridge between Evidence Store, Trust Score Service, MCP tools, Governance Vision, Observability, and future enterprise automation.
