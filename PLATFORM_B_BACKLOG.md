# Platform B Backlog

## Backlog rule

Platform B v1 is frozen. Do not add new concepts directly into v1 architecture.

New ideas must be classified as:

- v1.1 candidate
- v1.2 candidate
- v2 candidate
- Research-only candidate
- AEBOK teaching candidate
- Demo enhancement candidate

## v1.1 candidates

Packaging improvements, copy cleanup, route polish, screenshot organization, and release-note refinements.

## v1.2 candidates

Non-architectural usability enhancements that do not change the locked lifecycle or v1 capability architecture.

## v2 candidates

New lifecycle ideas, major modules, new dashboards, new architecture, or foundational conceptual expansion.

## Research-only candidates

PhD framing, grant language, academic papers, and Assurance Engineering theory development.

## AEBOK teaching candidates

Case studies, teaching diagrams, terminology, and practitioner exercises.
