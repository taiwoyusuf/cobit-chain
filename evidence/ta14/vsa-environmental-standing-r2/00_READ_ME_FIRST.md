# TA-14 / VSA Environmental Validation Standing — Founding Demonstration Package R2

**Working title:** VSA Environmental Validation Standing Under Hidden Physical Context Drift  
**Status:** Participant synthetic evidence package submission candidate — not yet admitted by TA-14  
**Purpose:** Provide one bounded, reproducible proposition for independent challenge.

## Bounded proposition

Can historically supportable environmental qualification evidence remain authentic while becoming insufficient to establish current validation standing after a material physical/context change alters measurement representativeness or evidence applicability — even when instruments remain calibrated and monitored values remain within their original limits — and can VSA selectively restore only those propositions for which new evidence actually re-establishes standing?

## Core distinction

`historical qualification != current validation standing`

The package is deliberately synthetic. It does not claim regulatory compliance, real-facility qualification, safety, product quality, or TA-14 Environmental Integrity functionality.

## Package sequence

1. Baseline evidence
2. Immaterial change
3. Material physical/context change
4. Proposition graph
5. Deterministic materiality determination
6. Standing transitions
7. Partial new evidence
8. Selective restoration
9. Final restoration evidence
10. Replayable expected findings

## Standing vocabulary

- `SUPPORTABLE`
- `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`
- `NOT_ESTABLISHED`

Historical state is preserved separately from current standing. No state is rewritten retroactively.


## R2 hardening
Explicit proposition requirements, versioned qualification basis, materiality matrix, epistemic classes, inference trap, same-value/different-standing proof, deterministic closure.
