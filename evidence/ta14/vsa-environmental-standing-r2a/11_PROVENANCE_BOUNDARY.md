# 11 — Provenance and Architectural Boundary

The environmental application is VSA-specific.

Acknowledgment:

Boundary:
This package does not claim TA-14 Environmental Integrity mechanisms, institutional authority, execution permission, action receipts, or capability-versus-authority architecture.

The proposition tested here is narrower:

**Does previously sufficient validation evidence remain applicable to a current proposition after the physical/context relationship that made the evidence representative has changed?**

VSA scope in this package:
- historical qualification preservation;
- changed-condition materiality;
- current evidence applicability;
- proposition-specific standing;
- selective reassessment;
- selective evidence-based restoration;
- replay of the resulting standing sequence.
