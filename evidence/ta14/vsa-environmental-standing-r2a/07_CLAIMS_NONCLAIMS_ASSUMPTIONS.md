# 07 — Claims, Non-Claims, Assumptions

## Claims this synthetic demonstration is permitted to support if reproduced

1. VSA can preserve historical supportability while separately changing current standing.
2. VSA can distinguish an immaterial change from a material change relative to a defined proposition.
3. Evidence can remain authentic while its current applicability becomes insufficient.
4. A calibrated instrument and in-limit readings do not, by themselves, establish measurement representativeness.
5. Proposition-specific standing can change selectively rather than globally.
6. New evidence restores only the propositions whose current evidence requirements it actually satisfies.
7. The supplied deterministic rules and evidence surface can reproduce the expected standing sequence.

## Non-claims

This package does **not** establish:

- actual GMP compliance;
- actual cleanroom qualification;
- actual environmental safety;
- actual sterility assurance;
- actual product quality;
- validity of any real facility, BMS, EMS, sensor, instrument, or process;
- regulatory acceptance of the synthetic criteria;
- authority to operate, release, manufacture, compound, dispense, administer, or expose personnel;
- TA-14 Environmental Integrity functionality;
- universal validity of VSA;
- certification of VSA or COBIT-Chain;
- independent live reproduction unless TA-14 actually performs one;
- that calibration alone establishes representativeness;
- that environmental evidence alone establishes product disposition.

## Assumptions

1. All data are synthetic.
2. `E1` was genuinely sufficient for the T0 baseline by construction.
3. `C1` changes no relied-upon physical/context basis.
4. `C2` changes geometry/airflow sufficiently to trigger representativeness reassessment by rule.
5. `E2` addresses P3/P5 but intentionally not P6.
6. `E3` addresses P6 under the changed configuration.
7. P4 remains independent of the C2 change in this bounded scenario.
8. No hidden evidence exists outside the admitted package.
9. The evaluator applies only the declared deterministic rules.

## R2 additional bounded claims

8. Same numerical measurements can remain authentic while carrying different evidentiary standing after context change.
9. High-confidence inference does not by itself restore directly unestablished representativeness.
10. Current standing is evaluated against an explicit current qualification/evidence basis rather than file authenticity alone.
