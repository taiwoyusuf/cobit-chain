## 2. Native VSA proposition

Validation Standing Assurance asks whether the regulated basis for relying on a particular digital system for a defined intended use remains supportable under current evidence, configuration, dependencies, procedures, authority, and quality-system conditions.

The native architecture preserves these separations:

- historical validation status does not automatically establish current Validation Standing;
- Validation Standing is distinct from Authority Standing;
- Validation Standing is distinct from Action Admissibility;
- Action Admissibility is distinct from execution;
- execution is distinct from outcome;
- correction/remediation is distinct from recovery and re-closure of standing.

A historically defensible determination remains part of the historical record even when current reliance later becomes unsupported or requires reassessment.
