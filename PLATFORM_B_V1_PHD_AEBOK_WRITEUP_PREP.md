# Platform B v1 — PhD and AEBOK Write-Up Prep

## Research translation

Platform B v1 can be translated into Assurance Engineering research language as an operational trust infrastructure for AI-enabled regulated, cyber-physical, agentic, evidence-sensitive, and human-critical environments.

## Teaching translation

For AEBOK, Platform B v1 can be positioned as a practitioner-facing example of how assurance moves beyond policy, audit logging, and static governance into continuous operational trust.

## Core teaching claim

AI governance that cannot be evidenced in operation cannot be trusted in operation.
