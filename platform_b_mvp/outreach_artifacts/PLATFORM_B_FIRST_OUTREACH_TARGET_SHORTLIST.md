## Scoring criteria for targets

Use this simple fit score before contact:

| Criterion | Score |
|---|---|
| Strong research fit | 0-3 |
| Funding or grant relevance | 0-3 |
| IP protection relevance | 0-3 |
| Regulated AI relevance | 0-3 |

Maximum score: 15

Prioritize targets scoring 10 or higher.

## Suggested first outreach sequence

1. Technology transfer or innovation office for IP protection guidance.
2. PhD supervisor or faculty fit contact.
3. Research office or grant office for pilot funding.
4. Carefully selected industry innovation reviewer only after disclosure boundary is clear.

## First message rule

The first message should ask for a discussion.

It should not explain the full technical design.

It should not attach source code.

It should not disclose confidential or proprietary details.

## First meeting objective

The first meeting should answer:

- Is there research fit?
- Is there funding fit?
- Is there a faculty sponsor path?
- What is the correct IP protection process?
- Should the next step be invention disclosure, NDA, grant concept note, or faculty collaboration review?
