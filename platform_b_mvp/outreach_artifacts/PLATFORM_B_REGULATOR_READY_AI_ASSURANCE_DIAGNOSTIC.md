## Purpose

The Platform B Regulator-Ready AI Assurance Diagnostic helps organizations assess whether their AI systems, LLM use cases, agentic workflows, records, evidence, human oversight, and lifecycle controls are ready for regulated reliance, inspection, and operational trust.

The diagnostic is designed for regulated, evidence-sensitive, human-critical, pharmaceutical, healthcare, manufacturing, and enterprise AI environments.

## Protected doctrine

AI-ready is not enough.

Organizations need regulator-ready, evidence-ready, and reliance-ready AI.

## Positioning

This diagnostic does not copy or replace any existing unified information governance diagnostic.

Platform B's diagnostic is positioned around:

- assurance
- regulatory evidence
- execution legitimacy
- operational trust
- records defensibility
- human oversight evidence
- lifecycle continuity
- right-to-rely readiness

## Core diagnostic question

Can the organization prove that its AI-enabled systems, workflows, outputs, actions, evidence, records, and human oversight controls are ready for regulated reliance?

## Scoring model

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Not defined | The control or capability is not defined. |
| 1 | Defined in policy | The control exists in policy, guidance, or governance documents. |
| 2 | Implemented in workflow | The control is embedded in an operational workflow or system process. |
| 3 | Evidenced and auditable | The control produces evidence that can be reviewed or audited. |
| 4 | Continuously monitored | The control is monitored, measured, or reviewed on an ongoing basis. |
| 5 | Regulator-ready / reliance-ready | The control can support regulated reliance, inspection, reconstruction, and defensible decision-making. |

## Diagnostic domains

The diagnostic assesses 12 domains:

1. AI Use Case & Context

# 1. AI Use Case & Context

## Diagnostic intent

Determine whether AI use cases are clearly defined, classified, owned, and linked to a context of use.

## Assessment questions

- Is each AI use case registered?
- Is the context of use defined?
- Is the intended user or workflow identified?
- Is the AI output type defined?
- Is the risk class defined?
- Is the business owner identified?
- Is the technical owner identified?
- Is the regulated impact understood?
- Is the human role defined?
- Is the lifecycle stage documented?

## Evidence examples

- AI use case register
- context-of-use statement
- risk classification
- owner assignment
- workflow map
- lifecycle-stage record
- approval record
- use-case intake form

## Domain score

0 to 5

# Diagnostic output

## Recommended output sections

A completed diagnostic should produce:

1. Executive readiness summary
2. Domain-by-domain score
3. Top assurance gaps
4. Regulator-ready evidence gaps
5. Records lifecycle gaps
6. Human oversight gaps
7. Agentic action-control gaps
8. Execution legitimacy gaps
9. Operational trust / right-to-rely position
10. Recommended remediation roadmap

## Suggested score interpretation

| Average score | Interpretation |
|---:|---|
| 0.0 - 0.9 | Not AI-assurance ready |
| 1.0 - 1.9 | Policy-defined but weakly operationalized |
| 2.0 - 2.9 | Operational but weakly evidenced |
| 3.0 - 3.9 | Evidenced and auditable, but not continuously assured |
| 4.0 - 4.4 | Continuously monitored, near regulator-ready |
| 4.5 - 5.0 | Regulator-ready / reliance-ready |
