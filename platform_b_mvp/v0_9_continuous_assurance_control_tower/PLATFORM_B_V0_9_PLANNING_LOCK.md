## Version

Platform B v0.9

## Working title

Continuous Assurance Control Tower

## Planning status

Planning lock.

## Core thesis

Platform B v0.9 extends the regulated action admissibility engine into a continuous assurance control tower.

The control tower does not only ask whether one action can proceed now.

It shows whether the regulated site, workflow, evidence mesh, exception state, reviewer load, and admissibility pattern remain trustworthy over time.

## Core doctrine

Assurance is not a one-time gate. It is a living operational state.

## Carry-forward doctrines

- The device senses. Platform B assures.
- The future regulated site will not only be monitored. It will be assurance-sensed.
- A regulated action is not trusted because one signal is green. It is trusted when the evidence mesh agrees.
- The environment is part of the evidence.
- Quiet when trusted. Alert when trust is at risk. Evidence when action is challenged.

## Relationship to v0.8

v0.8 defined the Regulated Action Admissibility Engine.

v0.9 defines the control tower that observes admissibility patterns across actions, rooms, equipment, people, procedures, time windows, exceptions, evidence packets, and reviewer decisions.

## v0.9 control tower question

Is the regulated operation continuously trustworthy, based on the current and recent state of evidence, exceptions, admissibility decisions, reviewers, and operational context?

It does not claim validated GMP use, clinical use, patient use, batch release support, shipment release support, regulatory submission use, autonomous execution, or production compliance.

## IP positioning

Platform B v0.9 continues the protected Platform B evolution from ambient witnesses, evidence mesh, assurance-sensed runtime, and admissibility gating into continuous assurance governance.
