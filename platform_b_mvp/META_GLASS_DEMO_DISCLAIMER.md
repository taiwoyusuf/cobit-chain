## Purpose

This disclaimer defines the limits of the Platform B v0.3 Meta Glass Wearable Field Demo.

## Demo boundary

Platform B v0.3 is a non-confidential hardware-assisted field demo.

It shows how wearable-captured context can support operational trust evidence.

It does not claim regulated, production, GMP, clinical, validation, patient, customer, or regulatory submission readiness.

## Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses boundary

Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses are used only as a demo capture device.

The glasses are not the Platform B product.

The glasses are not used for autonomous execution.

The glasses are not used to make regulated decisions.

The glasses are not used to capture patient, customer, confidential company, GMP, validation, or restricted operational information.

## Platform B boundary

Platform B v0.3 receives demo evidence uploaded by the user.

Platform B v0.3 may create a demo context-assurance signal, action admissibility result, operational trust update, and exportable evidence summary.

These outputs are for demonstration only.

They must not be used for real operational decisions.
