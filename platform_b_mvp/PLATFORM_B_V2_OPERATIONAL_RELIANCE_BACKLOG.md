# 1. Platform B v2 Candidate — Operational Reliance Infrastructure

## Purpose

Operational Reliance Infrastructure determines whether an AI output, recommendation, workflow, agent action, or AI-enabled decision deserves reliance under current evidence, authority, policy, context, and recovery conditions.

## Core reliance question

Can this AI output, recommendation, workflow, or action be relied on under the current evidence, authority, policy, context, and recovery state?

## v2 capability — Reliance Gate

A pre-reliance decision point.

Before a human, workflow, system, or regulator accepts an AI output, Platform B determines:

- rely
- rely with conditions
- human review required
- do not rely
- suspend / escalate

Protected phrase:

The future is not just AI that acts. It is AI whose outputs earn the right to be relied on.

## v2 capability — Reliance Packet

Reliance Packet is a core product primitive.

Every AI output or material action generates a portable evidence object containing:

- AI use case
- context of use
- model / agent / tool version
- data lineage
- policy applied
- authority boundary
- human reviewer
- evidence source
- confidence and limitation
- action admissibility
- recovery path
- reliance decision
- timestamp
- accountable owner

Protected phrase:

No Reliance Packet, no regulated reliance.

## v2 capability — Reliance Graph

A graph showing how an AI output became something the business relied on.

It connects:

- prompt
- source data
- retrieval
- model
- tool call
- human review
- action
- system-of-record entry
- evidence
- downstream business decision

Protected phrase:

The AI answer is not the endpoint. The reliance chain is the regulated object.

## v2 capability — Reliance-Level Classification

Not just AI risk classification.

Classify outputs by reliance level:

- Level 0 — Informational only
- Level 1 — Human-considered
- Level 2 — Human-reviewed
- Level 3 — Workflow-relied
- Level 4 — System-of-record relied
- Level 5 — Regulated / patient / safety relied

Protected phrase:

The same AI answer becomes more dangerous as reliance increases.

## v2 capability — Right-to-Rely Score

Do not call this only a trust score.

A regulator or executive does not only ask whether they trust the AI output.

They ask whether they have the right to rely on it.

Platform B v2 may calculate:

- evidence completeness
- context match
- authority match
- human oversight quality
- source currency
- policy compliance
- action admissibility
- recovery feasibility
- system-of-record impact
- residual risk

Protected phrase:

The future of AI assurance is not confidence scoring. It is right-to-rely scoring.

# 2. Platform B v2 Candidate — Forward-Deployed AI Assurance

## Purpose

Forward-Deployed AI Assurance addresses the enterprise AI deployment model where hyperscalers, AI labs, consultants, or embedded engineering teams enter customer environments to co-design, deploy, and continuously improve AI systems.

The assurance question is not only whether AI was deployed successfully.

The assurance question is:

Can the enterprise prove that the AI capability, workflow logic, operational evidence, governance boundary, IP boundary, security posture, accountability model, and continuous improvement process remain trustworthy after external deployment support?

## v2 capability — Forward-Deployed AI Assurance

Assurance for AI systems built or improved by embedded external engineering teams.

## v2 capability — Enterprise AI Capability Ownership Assurance

Verifies that the enterprise owns or controls the resulting AI workflows, evidence, operational knowledge, configuration, and improvement logic.

## v2 capability — Embedded Vendor Boundary Assurance

Defines what external AI engineers can access, change, learn, export, reuse, or retain.

## v2 capability — Co-Development Evidence Ledger

Captures who changed what, why, when, under whose authority, using which data, model, workflow, and approval path.

## v2 capability — AI Transformation Evidence Assurance

Demonstrates whether AI transformation produced measurable, governed, and auditable operational outcomes.

## v2 capability — Customer Intelligence Sovereignty Assurance

Verifies that enterprise data, workflow knowledge, operating intelligence, and IP-sensitive improvements remain protected.

This capability is especially important for forward-deployed engineering models where external teams help build or improve customer AI systems.

It proves that the customer still controls:

- workflow logic
- domain knowledge
- improvement patterns
- evidence records
- prompts / configurations
- model choices
- data boundaries
- operational playbooks
- internal capability

Protected phrase:

Forward deployment accelerates capability. Assurance preserves ownership.

## v2 capability — Post-Deployment Self-Sufficiency Assurance

Verifies whether the customer can operate, monitor, change, recover, and govern the AI system after the external team leaves.

## v2 capability — Continuous Improvement Evidence Assurance

Captures evidence that AI systems are improved under controlled, traceable, and approved conditions.

## v2 capability — Forward-Deployed AI Assurance Pack

A package for any hyperscaler, AI-lab, consulting-led, or embedded-engineering AI deployment.

It includes:

- pre-engagement boundary
- data / IP access rules
- co-development evidence ledger
- change history
- AI workflow ownership
- customer self-sufficiency proof
- post-deployment monitoring
- exit / transfer package
- regulator / audit-ready summary

Protected phrase:

The AI deployment is not complete until the customer can govern it without the deployer.

## v2 protected doctrine

The risk of forward-deployed AI is not only vendor access.

It is losing control of the operational intelligence that makes the enterprise unique.

# 6. Platform B v2 Candidate — Reality-to-Reliance Continuity Assurance

## Purpose

Reality-to-Reliance Continuity Assurance verifies that the real-world condition, user need, operational signal, regulatory context, equipment state, patient/business risk, or workflow problem that initiated an AI-enabled process remains traceable and undistorted through requirements, prompts, retrieval, model output, human review, decision, action, and evidence.

The v2 question is:

Did operational reality survive the AI workflow before the organization relied on the result?

## v2 capability — Reality Continuity Assurance

Verifies that the original operational reality remains traceable across each AI-enabled handoff.

## v2 capability — Translation Integrity Assurance

Detects distortion when a real-world problem becomes a requirement, prompt, query, model output, decision, or executed action.

## v2 capability — Context-to-Action Fidelity

Measures whether the final action still reflects the original context, constraints, and risk state.

## v2 capability — Prompt-to-Reality Traceability

Links prompts and AI outputs back to the real operational condition they were supposed to represent.

## v2 capability — Operational Meaning Preservation

Ensures that workflow meaning is not lost as AI transforms signals into summaries, recommendations, tasks, or actions.

## v2 capability — Execution Authority Preclearance

Validates execution authority before AI, automation, code, or agent workflow is allowed to act.

## v2 capability — Operational Substrate Readiness Assurance

Verifies that data, knowledge articles, procedures, catalog items, ownership, escalation paths, and records are clean enough before autonomy is introduced.

## v2 capability — Agent Operating Instruction Assurance

Treats agent instructions like controlled procedure manuals:

- versioned
- tested
- reversible
- approved
- evidence-linked

## v2 capability — Control-Layer Interoperability Assurance

Maps how Platform B complements enterprise AI control layers, cloud AI deployments, data controls, and enterprise workflow systems.

## v2 capability — Reality Drift Signal

Detects when AI outputs, workflows, or decisions begin drifting away from the real-world condition they were intended to represent.

## Protected doctrine

The future of AI assurance is not only proving that AI acted safely.

It is proving that AI acted on reality, not distortion.

## v2 positioning

Platform B v2 should not compete with AWS, Microsoft, ServiceNow, Oracle, or enterprise workflow systems.

It should sit above and across them as:

The independent continuity and reliance layer that proves AI-enabled work preserved reality, earned reliance, and executed under valid authority.

# 7. Platform B v2 Candidate Product — Reliance Packet API

## Purpose

The Platform B Reliance Packet API is a small attachable product candidate.

It generates a Reliance Packet every time an enterprise AI agent produces a regulated output, performs a material action, or changes a workflow.

## Minimum candidate endpoints

- POST /reliance/packet
- POST /reliance/gate
- POST /action/admissibility
- POST /evidence/attach
- GET /reliance/report/{id}
- GET /sovereignty/passport/{deployment_id}

## Why this product may matter

Enterprise AI deployments need standardized proof that AI outputs, workflows, and actions are safe enough to rely on.

Forward-deployed AI engagements need evidence that deployments are governed and customer-self-sufficient.

Enterprise workflow platforms need external assurance evidence beyond inventory, dashboards, and control towers.

Pharma, healthcare, manufacturing, and regulated enterprises need audit-ready evidence, not just workflow automation.

Regulators need traceable reliance, not just AI usage logs.
