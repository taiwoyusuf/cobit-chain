## First-tier tracks

Platform B1 / MVP2 first-tier tracks are:

1. Compound Pharmacy
2. IRLT / Radiopharma Operations
3. DSCSA Evidence Integrity & Exception Assurance

These are not casual backlog ideas. They are the priority commercialization and demonstration tracks.

## Track 1 — Compound Pharmacy

Compound Pharmacy is one of the first commercial target groups.

The compounding-pharmacy module is already built and should be packaged, positioned, and connected to COBIT-Chain's evidence-integrity foundation.

Primary assurance focus:

- formula and preparation evidence
- material and lot traceability
- operator accountability
- beyond-use-date evidence
- quality review
- audit readiness
- AI-generated content governance
- hashing and rehashing of evidence

## Track 2 — IRLT / Radiopharma Operations

IRLT / radiopharma operations is a first-tier demonstration and commercialization track.

It should not be treated as merely in scope. It is one of the early priority tracks.

Primary assurance focus:

- radiopharma manufacturing readiness
- equipment and CI readiness
- controlled operational handoffs
- quality and release dependency checks
- ServiceNow operational assurance

## Track 3 — DSCSA Evidence Integrity & Exception Assurance

DSCSA Evidence Integrity & Exception Assurance is a top-tier supply-chain and evidence-integrity extension.

## Doctrine

COBIT-Chain does not replace source systems.

COBIT-Chain does not release product.

COBIT-Chain does not approve GMP work.

COBIT-Chain does not replace Quality Unit review.

COBIT-Chain does not become MES, LIMS, ERP, eQMS, VRS, EPCIS, or ServiceNow.

COBIT-Chain verifies whether the evidence chain, workflow state, identity, authority, AI output, and dependency state can be trusted.

Platform B1 evaluates.

Thread D2 displays.

Official records remain in source systems.

Humans remain accountable.

Quality Unit remains authoritative.
