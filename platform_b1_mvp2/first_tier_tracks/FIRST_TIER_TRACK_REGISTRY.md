# Platform B1 MVP2 First-Tier Track Registry

Status: LOCKED FIRST-TIER TRACKS

This registry defines the first-tier commercialization and demonstration tracks for COBIT-Chain / Platform B1 MVP2.

It prevents uncontrolled scope expansion by placing Compound Pharmacy, IRLT / Radiopharma Operations, and DSCSA Evidence Integrity & Exception Assurance into a clear first-tier structure.

## First-tier tracks

1. Compound Pharmacy
2. IRLT / Radiopharma Operations
3. DSCSA Evidence Integrity & Exception Assurance

These tracks are priority commercialization and demonstration tracks.

They are not generic backlog items.

## Track 1 — Compound Pharmacy

Commercial position:

One of the first commercial target groups.

Module state:

Existing module to package.

Primary question:

Can a compounded preparation workflow be trusted, reconstructed, reviewed, and defended from formula through preparation, quality review, and evidence package?

Assurance focus:

- formula evidence
- preparation evidence
- material and lot traceability
- beyond-use-date evidence
- operator accountability
- quality review
- audit readiness
- AI-generated content governance
- hashing and rehashing of evidence

Governed AI examples:

- AI-assisted compounding record review
- AI-generated SOP or procedure draft review
- AI-assisted discrepancy summary
- AI-assisted audit package preparation
- AI-assisted training or readiness checklist

AI boundary:

AI may assist review, summary, triage, and package preparation.

AI may not release compounded preparation, replace pharmacist review, replace Quality Unit review, approve deviations, approve investigations, or create uncontrolled GMP instructions for direct use.

Example outputs:

- COMPOUNDING EVIDENCE SEALED
- HASH VERIFIED
- REHASH REQUIRED
- MATERIAL LOT TRACEABLE
- BUD EVIDENCE REVIEW REQUIRED
- FORMULA EVIDENCE MISSING
- AI CONTENT REVIEW REQUIRED
- QUALITY REVIEW REQUIRED
- COMPOUNDING PACKAGE DEFENSIBLE

## Track 2 — IRLT / Radiopharma Operations

Commercial position:

One of the first-tier demonstration and commercialization tracks.

Module state:

Priority extension track.

Primary question:

Can IRLT / radiopharma operational execution be trusted, reconstructed, and defended across equipment, CI readiness, operational handoffs, quality dependencies, and evidence state?

Assurance focus:

- radiopharma manufacturing readiness
- equipment and CI readiness
- controlled operational handoffs
- quality and release dependency checks
- ServiceNow operational assurance
- RAMAT Vision display
- AI governance around operational recommendations
- hashing and rehashing of evidence

Governed AI examples:

- AI-assisted operational readiness summary
- AI-assisted CI and evidence gap detection
- AI-assisted change or incident impact summary
- AI-assisted quality dependency review
- AI-assisted RAMAT Vision field guidance

AI boundary:

AI may assist with summarization, dependency detection, review routing, and field guidance.

AI may not release product, approve GMP work, override ServiceNow, override QMS, override LIS, override MES, replace source-system records, replace Quality Unit review, or authorize regulated execution without human accountability.

Example outputs:

- IRLT EVIDENCE SEALED
- HASH VERIFIED
- REHASH REQUIRED
- EQUIPMENT CI READY
- CI READINESS GAP
- CONTROLLED HANDOFF VERIFIED
- QUALITY DEPENDENCY BLOCKED
- RAMAT VISION DISPLAY READY
- AI RECOMMENDATION REVIEW REQUIRED
- IRLT PACKAGE DEFENSIBLE

## Track 3 — DSCSA Evidence Integrity & Exception Assurance

Commercial position:

Top-tier supply-chain and evidence-integrity extension.

Module state:

Project immediately as a top-tier extension.

Primary question:

Can serialized prescription-drug supply-chain evidence be trusted, reconstructed, and defended across DSCSA trading-partner handoffs, EPCIS evidence, VRS response, exception resolution, and AI-assisted triage?

Assurance focus:

- serialized product identity
- EPCIS evidence integrity
- VRS response assurance
- authorized trading partner trust
- exception classification
- quarantine and release-readiness support
- AI-governed exception triage
- hashing and rehashing of supply-chain evidence

Governed AI examples:

- AI-assisted DSCSA exception classification
- AI-assisted EPCIS mismatch summary
- AI-assisted trading partner outreach draft
- AI-assisted quarantine or review routing
- AI-assisted exception trend analysis

AI boundary:

AI may classify, summarize, route, and recommend review steps.

AI may not release product, override quarantine, declare DSCSA compliance, declare product legitimate or illegitimate without responsible human review, or replace required regulatory, quality, or trading-partner processes.

Example outputs:

- DSCSA EVIDENCE SEALED
- HASH VERIFIED
- REHASH REQUIRED
- EPCIS FILE MISMATCH
- SERIAL EVENT CHANGED
- CHAIN OF CUSTODY GAP
- TRADING PARTNER NOT VERIFIED
- VRS RESPONSE MISSING
- AGGREGATION CONFLICT
- DSCSA EXCEPTION DETECTED
- AI EXCEPTION CLASSIFICATION GENERATED
- HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED
- QUARANTINE REQUIRED
- EXCEPTION RESOLUTION EVIDENCE COMPLETE
- EXCEPTION NOT DEFENSIBLE
