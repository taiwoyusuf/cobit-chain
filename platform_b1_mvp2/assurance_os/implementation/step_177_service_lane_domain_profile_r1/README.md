# Step 177 — Service Lane & Domain Profile Productization R1

## Purpose

Make the commercial/product boundary explicit without allowing commercial status to contaminate technical assurance standing.

## Four independent service lanes

1. `CONTINUOUS_ASSURANCE` — ongoing standing, evidence, dependency, change and revalidation.
2. `BOUNDED_ASSURANCE_REVIEW` — tightly scoped review of an agent, workflow, physical/environmental route, evidence chain or consequential action.
3. `ARCHITECTURE_EVIDENCE_PROVENANCE` — identities, versions, demonstrations, claims/non-claims and chronology.
4. `ENTERPRISE_ASSURANCE_DEPLOYMENT` — multi-agent, multi-system, multi-domain Platform B1 operation using shared assurance core plus domain profiles.

## Non-evidentiary commercial invariant

Payment, registration, subscription, review purchase, or commercial participation MUST NOT establish or increase technical assurance standing.

Technical standing remains dependent on admissible evidence, applicable criteria, valid dependencies, current authority and verified evaluation.

## Domain synchronization

Step 175 Assurance Invariants R1 and Step 176 Physical Evidence Standing R2 are now explicitly mapped for reuse by:

- RAMAT Vision
- Clinical Trial Assurance
- Radiopharma Assurance
- ALARA / Radiological Operations Assurance
- CompoundSafe
- LabTrust
- DSCSA Assurance

The mapping does not duplicate the shared engines and does not convert domain-specific roadmap items into implemented capabilities.

## Maturity boundary

This step implements and unit-tests the service-lane technical/commercial separation and records domain inheritance mappings. It does not claim full UI productization, pricing, enterprise deployment, regulatory validation, independent reproduction, or production qualification.
