# Evidence Before Trust

Step: 174C

Status: controlled local static implementation.

Planning assumption: true.

Synthetic only: true.

Public-source validation required: true.

Generated evidence created: false.

Generated evidence is reserved for Step 174D.

## Purpose

Define future cross-family doctrine, scope, authority, decision, evidence, maturity, and validation controls.

## Authority boundary

- Platform B1 remains the assurance decision engine.
- Accountable humans remain responsible for regulated decisions.
- Official records remain in external source systems.
- Source-state disagreement is represented and is not silently reconciled.
- No-Bind Governance activates when evidence or authority is insufficient.

## RAMAT Vision boundary

- RAMAT Vision may display, witness, request, and support review.
- RAMAT Vision may not approve, release, override, write back, reconcile source states, resolve holds, or bind regulated action.

## Decision contract

- Outcomes: ALLOW, HOLD, NO-BIND, DENY, FAIL-CLOSED.
- Precedence: DENY, FAIL-CLOSED, NO-BIND, HOLD, ALLOW.

## Data and claim boundary

- No PHI, patient data, clinical-subject data, real trial data, real pharmacy data, real traceability data, company production data, or real regulated records.
- Legal, regulatory, clinical, safety, standards, jurisdictional, commercial, and market language remains a planning assumption until authoritative public-source validation is completed.
