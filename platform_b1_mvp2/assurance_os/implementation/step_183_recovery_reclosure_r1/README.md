# Step 183 — Recovery / Re-closure / Return-to-Reliance Standing R1

## Purpose

Step 183 closes the bounded post-disturbance gap after Step 182 by keeping four propositions separate:

`RECOVERY != DISPOSITION_CLOSURE != RECLOSURE != RETURN_TO_RELIANCE`

A stopped execution is not evidence of reversal. Recovery within a declared scope is not evidence that all governed obligations are closed. Closure of a condition is not evidence that current world state, criteria, authority, and evidence support return to reliance. Later recovery never rewrites the historical failure, divergence, or non-establishment that triggered recovery.

## Composition

Step 183 composes with, and does not replace:

- Step 175 Shared Assurance Invariants;
- Step 176 Physical Evidence Standing;
- Step 177 Service-Lane / Domain-Profile mapping;
- Step 178 typed Recovery Standing and Disposition Standing;
- Step 179 route-complete Boundary Enforcement Non-Bypass;
- Step 180 Execution-Time Revalidation;
- Step 181 Atomic Evaluated-to-Committed Binding; and
- Step 182 Commit / Execution / Outcome Correspondence.

It does not create a duplicate CAPA, deviation, OOS/OOT, recall, radiation-release, pharmacist-release, patient-safety, environmental, or physical-witness engine.

## Bounded evaluation sequence

1. Preserve the prior Step 182 outcome/failure/divergence fact exactly as established.
2. Require `RECOVERED_WITHIN_DECLARED_SCOPE` from the applicable recovery evaluation.
3. Require the governed disposition to be `CLOSED_WITH_EVIDENCE` where closure is required.
4. Identify residual obligations and prevent re-closure while any blocking obligation remains open.
5. Preserve non-blocking residual obligations rather than silently deleting them at re-entry.
6. Re-establish current world/physical correspondence where applicable.
7. Re-establish current criteria/definition, current authority, and current required evidence.
8. Establish a new post-recovery/re-entry baseline.
9. Where the domain requires it, require independent outcome or physical-state re-verification.
10. Only then may `RECLOSURE_SUPPORTABLE` be returned. This remains a non-binding assurance result.

## Core doctrine

- `STOPPED != RECOVERED`
- `RECOVERED != CLOSED`
- `CLOSED != RECLOSED`
- `RECLOSED != BINDING AUTHORITY`
- `LATER_RECOVERY != RETROACTIVE_REPAIR_OF_PRIOR_FAILURE`
- `CUSTODY_TRANSFER != OBLIGATION_CLOSURE`
- `OPEN_NONBLOCKING_OBLIGATION != ERASED_OBLIGATION`
- `HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE_VALID != CURRENT_REENTRY_STANDING`

## Domain intent

- **Clinical Trial:** site/patient/safety remediation and return-to-reliance without erasing SAE/CAPA or protocol-history facts.
- **CompoundSafe:** deviation/CAPA and environmental/process re-closure before pharmacist/quality reliance resumes.
- **Radiopharma:** contamination/process/product/dose pathway recovery and re-entry with current physical and authority evidence.
- **ALARA:** stop-work, contamination/area recovery, re-entry, and residual radiological obligations.
- **DSCSA:** suspect/illegitimate product investigation, quarantine, recall/recovery, and release-from-quarantine closure.
- **LabTrust:** OOS/OOT/investigation/corrected-result closure without erasing the original result or audit trail.
- **RAMAT/Niagara:** physical-context recovery, alarm/actuator recovery, and new-world baseline before downstream automation inherits prior reliance.

## Authority boundary

Step 183 does not grant regulatory, medical, pharmacist, sponsor, investigator, quality, RSO/HP/AU, trading-partner, analyst/reviewer, or other institutional authority. Authorized humans/institutions remain the source of binding decisions.
