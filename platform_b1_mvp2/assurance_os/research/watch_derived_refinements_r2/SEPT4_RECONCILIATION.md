## D. Direct effect on the currently prioritized research lanes

### Runtime Residual-Consequence Assurance

R2 adds five useful guardrails around the already-filed mechanism:

1. **Applicability Exclusion Standing** prevents a residual-support requirement from disappearing by unsupported `NOT_APPLICABLE` classification.
2. **Condition Coverage Standing** prevents the residual evaluation from pretending its dependency universe is complete.
3. **Observation Cadence Standing** asks whether the material change could have occurred undetected before the realization boundary.
4. **Material Change Reachability** proves whether the changed condition could actually affect the governing route before further consequence materialized.
5. **Evidence Failure Independence** prevents apparent independent reclosure evidence from sharing one hidden failure path.

These additions strengthen the experiment but do not replace the residual-consequence mechanism itself.

### Revocation / Effect Closure

The most useful R2 additions are:

- Authority Basis Standing: a valid delegation chain still needs legitimate terminal authority.
- Material Change Reachability: revocation must reach the gate before consequence.
- Condition Coverage Standing: closure claims remain bounded by the routes/dependencies actually represented.

The stronger future experiment should distinguish registered-lineage closure from effect-path closure and from the standing of the coverage basis used to make the closure claim.

### Canonical Audit R3

The most useful R2 additions are:

- Applicability Exclusion Standing: materially applicable branches cannot disappear through unsupported exclusion.
- Partial Evidence Bound Soundness: incomplete valid evidence cannot produce an architecture-wide positive claim.
- Condition Coverage Standing: strongest-positive claims are capped when the examined dependency universe remains open.

Examiner qualification and test-harness integrity remain the next methodology hardening layer.

### RAMAT Vision

The most useful R2 additions are:

- Observation Cadence Standing
- Condition Coverage Standing
- Evidence Failure Independence
- Material Change Reachability

RAMAT remains witness-only. None of these functions authorize release, treatment, disposition, stop-work, or other binding regulated action.

## E. Important regulatory/physical validation patterns preserved from the supplied material

The watch corpus contributes strong domain examples without requiring new modules:

- actual physical exposure boundary can outrank nominal zone labels;
- calibration may be valid while destroying historical evidence if custody is not preserved;
- prescribed radiopharmaceutical activity does not prove delivered/localized activity;
- a real sensor signal may be mis-explained by an incomplete inference model;
- missing evidence must not silently become a numeric zero or negative fact;
- administrative qualification approval can conflict with failing physical evidence;
- a valid test protocol can still use an acceptance criterion incapable of rejecting physical failure;
- successful cross-version communication does not establish semantic/functional equivalence;
- an observing device can disturb the physical system it is supposed to establish;
- a digital twin allowed to affect a physical process needs current interface/correspondence standing;
- physical outcome must remain distinct from digital command/record status.

These patterns should be used as adversarial scenarios and publication examples, not as claims that the external sources implemented COBIT-Chain.
