# Aug. 31, 2026 Watch Reconciliation

Status: **EXPERIMENTAL / BRANCH-ONLY**

This reconciliation incorporates only generic assurance problems that can be implemented independently without importing another architecture's named ontology, sequence, or authority model.

## Newly implemented bounded evaluators

### Shared assurance / Platform B1 experiments

1. **Refusal Survivability Standing**
   - Extends Human Intervention / Human Authority rather than creating a new family.
   - `VETO_EXISTS != VETO_IS_PRACTICALLY_EXERCISABLE`.
   - Tests coercion/penalty exposure, independent escalation, preservation of refusal, and alternate-route bypass.

2. **Repairability Routing Standing**
   - Extends Consequence Classification + Action Admissibility + Governed Recovery.
   - `FAST_EXECUTION_CAPABILITY != FAST_EXECUTION_ADMISSIBILITY`.
   - Routes to `MACHINE_SPEED`, `HUMAN_CONFIRMATION`, or `HOLD` based on repairability evidence.

3. **Intent Constitution Artifact Standing**
   - Evidence artifact, not a new capability family.
   - Requires explicit objective, authorized/prohibited outcomes, negative boundaries, assumptions, context, delegation limits, escalation conditions, success/failure definitions, owner, version, and review trigger as applicable.
   - `AGENT_INSTRUCTION_EXISTS != GOVERNED_INTENT_ESTABLISHED`.

4. **Consequence Incapacity Challenge Pattern**
   - Test methodology, not runtime authority.
   - Requires route-complete challenge of normal, alternate, stale, replay, delegated, and bypass routes as applicable.
   - `BLOCK_LOGGED != CONSEQUENCE_PREVENTED`; `PROOF_SURFACE != PROOF`.

5. **Digital Proxy Non-Authority**
   - Extends Identity / Consent / Human Authority.
   - Person-models, inferred preferences, behavioral predictions, persistent profiles, and AI proxies cannot create or enlarge authority without an attributable current grant.

6. **Evidence Plane Survivability**
   - Extends Independent Evidence Plane Standing.
   - Required evidence-service outage cannot silently fall back to assumption or unauthorized cache.
   - Later service restoration is not retroactive contemporaneous proof.

7. **Lifecycle Exit / Retirement Standing**
   - Extends lifecycle / revocation / recovery rather than creating a new foundation.
   - Retirement is incomplete while consequence-bearing residual routes or unresolved duties remain.
   - Historical records must remain preserved while active lifecycle authority is closed.

8. **Persistent Memory Admissibility + Retrieval Custody**
   - Extends provenance/current-standing controls.
   - `MEMORY_VALID_WHEN_WRITTEN != MEMORY_ADMISSIBLE_NOW`.
   - Requires provenance, source/current authority scope, retention state, mutation lineage, retrieval custody, and current context applicability.

9. **Definition / Evidence Correspondence**
   - Extends Criteria Standing and continuing reliance.
   - A definition/version change may break current correspondence without making historical evidence false.

10. **Outcome-to-Next-Cycle Inheritance Standing**
    - Extends Outcome Correspondence / reconstruction into recursive operation.
    - A prior successful action may create a new physical/digital world state; prior authorization does not automatically carry into the next cycle.

11. **Measurement Semantic Standing**
    - Extends Measurement Integrity.
    - Numeric value, quantity/measurand, unit, scale, instrument mode, object/location, time, and interpretation basis are jointly material to consequential use.
    - `VALUE_PRESENT != DECISION_GRADE_MEASUREMENT`.

12. **Measurement Context Representativeness**
    - Extends Measurement Standing / representativeness.
    - Calibration does not establish field representativeness when sampling path, geometry, visibility, continuity, or acquisition intrusiveness is unqualified.

### Publication / external-review governance

13. **Lineage Challenge Record**
    - Research/publication control, not runtime.
    - Evaluates alleged parent artifact, chronology, artifact-level correspondence, evidence of access, derivation route, and independent comparison.
    - `CHRONOLOGY != DERIVATION != AUTHORSHIP != OWNERSHIP`.

14. **Architecture Influence Provenance Ledger**
    - Freezes pre-review artifact, dated external input, pre-existing mechanism, subsequent change, relationship class, attribution, authorship boundary, and ownership non-claim.
    - Relationship classes: `PRE_EXISTING`, `REVIEW_INFLUENCED`, `INDEPENDENTLY_DERIVED`, `JOINTLY_DEVELOPED`, `ATTRIBUTION_AGREED`, `ATTRIBUTION_UNRESOLVED`.

15. **Demonstration Participation Record**
    - External-examination charter.
    - Freezes subject/version, proposition, non-claims, evidence boundary, acceptance criteria, reviewer independence, IP/pre-existing rights, confidentiality, publication rights, claim ceiling, and correction/dispute path before evidence exchange.
    - `PARTICIPATION != AUTHORSHIP != OWNERSHIP != CERTIFICATION != AUTHORITY`.

16. **Framework-Neutral Qualification Envelope**
    - Cross-architecture review record.
    - Preserves native ontology/identity while comparing only a frozen falsifiable proposition and bounded evidence surface.
    - `QUALIFICATION != AUTHORITY`; `CROSS_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW != ONTOLOGY_CONVERGENCE`.

## Domain mapping

- **Clinical Trial:** consent/delegation, digitally derived endpoints, reviewer capacity, persistent context, obligation closure.
- **CompoundSafe:** sterile-process refusal, environmental representativeness, CAPA/remediation liveness, retirement of workflows/instruments.
- **Radiopharma:** unit/scale semantics, physical delivery/outcome, contextual release predicates, residual execution routes.
- **ALARA:** stop-work survivability, containment/re-entry obligations, instrument-context representativeness, residual authority after retirement.
- **DSCSA:** delegated ATP/partner authority, unresolved investigation duties, federated declarations later.
- **LabTrust:** measurement semantics, sampling-path representativeness, OOS/OOT obligations, persistent analytical context.
- **RAMAT / Niagara:** digital proxy non-authority, alarm/evidence-plane survivability, physical/digital recursion, measurement context, retirement of sensor/actuator workflows.

## Deferred / not silently promoted

The following remain roadmap or separate experiment families rather than current runtime authority: federated external standing declarations, assurance throughput/headroom benchmarking, OEM/data-center physical witness demonstrations, cross-architecture production interoperability, and any legal conclusion about inventorship, patent validity, priority, ownership, or derivation.
