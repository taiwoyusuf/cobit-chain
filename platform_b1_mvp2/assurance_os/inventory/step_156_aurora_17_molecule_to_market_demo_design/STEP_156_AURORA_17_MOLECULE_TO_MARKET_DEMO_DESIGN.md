# Step 156 - AURORA-17 Molecule-to-Market Flagship Demonstration Design

**AURORA-17 is a fictional radioligand-therapy candidate used only for controlled architecture, assurance, research, publication, and demonstration design.**

**Step 156 does not implement runtime workflows, manufacture a product, conduct a clinical trial, approve or release regulated work, use real patient data, or modify production systems.**

The flagship journey demonstrates how COBIT-Chain, the Universal Assurance Kernel, Regulated Object Identity, evidence integrity, hashing, rehashing, workflow dependency assurance, authority, human accountability, action admissibility, No-Bind Governance, and RAMAT Vision can connect one fictional molecule from discovery through post-market evidence.

## Locked architecture doctrine

- Platform B v1 remains ARCHITECTURE LOCKED.
- Thread D v1 remains ARCHITECTURE LOCKED.
- Platform B1 evaluates advanced assurance conditions without reopening Platform B v1.
- Thread D and Thread D2 remain DISPLAY / WITNESS ONLY.
- RAMAT Vision does not approve, release, override, resolve, execute, or become evidence authority.
- Qualified humans and authorized organizational roles remain accountable.
- Official records and executed actions remain in governed source systems.
- Admissibility is not execution.
- Silence is a governance state.
- Silence is not consent.
