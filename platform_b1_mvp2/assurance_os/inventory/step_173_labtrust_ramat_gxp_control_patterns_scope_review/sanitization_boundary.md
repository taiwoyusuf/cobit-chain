# Sanitization Boundary

Status: controlled static implementation
Data class: synthetic and non-production

## Allowed

- Synthetic demonstration identities and states
- Generalized vendor-neutral control objectives
- Local deterministic validators and test source

## Prohibited

- Internal procedure wording or identifiers
- Personal or patient information
- Real regulated or production records
- Credentials, secrets, certificates, endpoints, or access instructions
- Company-specific operational data
