Doctrine name: No-Bind Governance™ / Silence Is Not Consent Doctrine

## Master principle

Absence of human response must not be treated as authorization.

## Master sentence

No-Bind Governance™ means an AI-mediated action cannot bind an organization to consequence merely because no human responded. Silence must create a governed hold, escalation, and documented pause — not implied permission.

## Purpose

This registry preserves No-Bind Governance™ as a local doctrine entry for Platform B1 / MVP2 and Thread D2.

## Why this matters

In AI-mediated regulated workflows, silence, delay, unavailable approvers, missed alerts, and absent authority can be misread by systems as permission to continue.

No-Bind Governance™ prevents that failure mode.

The workflow is not trustworthy because no one objected.

The workflow is trustworthy only when authority, evidence, timing, accountability, and admissibility are present, or a governed hold / escalation state is created.

## Decision rule

If required authority is absent, invalid, unavailable, stale, unsupported by evidence, outside timing, or not accountable to a named person, role, or governed rule, the AI-mediated action is not admissible and must enter a governed hold or escalation state.
