Status: LOCKED AGENTIC AMBIENT AI VENDOR ASSURANCE PASSPORT ONLY

Passport name: Agentic & Ambient AI Vendor Assurance Passport™

## Core question

Can this AI-supported vendor system, documentation output, recommendation, agentic action, or workflow be trusted, reconstructed, reviewed, governed, and defended at the moment it matters?

## Why this is more than a checklist

A checklist asks: can we sign this vendor contract?

The Agentic & Ambient AI Vendor Assurance Passport™ asks:

Can this AI-supported action, note, recommendation, workflow, or vendor output be trusted, reconstructed, reviewed, governed, and defended at the moment it matters?

This registry is designed for pre-contract, pre-deployment, runtime, and audit-reconstruction assurance.

## Source problem

Agentic and ambient AI systems may be deployed before governance documentation is mature.

AI-generated documentation may be clinically or operationally influential even when humans remain accountable.

A signed or approved output is not defensible unless the organization can reconstruct:

- what the AI captured;
- what the AI generated;
- what the human saw;
- what the human changed;
- who approved it;
- what authority applied;
- when the review occurred;
- whether the evidence can be exported and defended.

Human-in-the-loop is not sufficient unless the human identity, qualification, review time, authority, evidence, and final accountability are provable.

Information governance is part of AI governance because AI behavior depends on governed information boundaries, provenance, purpose, retention, access, and release controls.

## Relationship to Platform A, Platform B1, and Thread D2

Platform A captures use case intent, vendor role, intended use, risk class, authority model, human review design, evidence requirements, data boundaries, and lifecycle governance design.

Platform B1 evaluates whether evidence, authority, review, accountability, vendor controls, lifecycle controls, and action admissibility are sufficient.

## Twelve assurance domains

### D01 — Intended Use and Consequence Classification

Question: What is the AI system intended to do, what decision or workflow can it influence, and what consequence could follow?

Required evidence:

- intended_use_statement
- prohibited_use_statement
- consequence_classification
- regulated_workflow_mapping
- patient_or_product_or_quality_impact_flag

### D02 — Regulatory Applicability Map

Question: Which regulatory, quality, privacy, safety, and contractual obligations apply to this AI use case?

Required evidence:

- hipaa_phi_assessment_if_healthcare
- gxp_assessment_if_pharma_or_quality
- eu_ai_act_applicability_if_relevant
- nist_ai_rmf_mapping
- iso_iec_42001_management_system_mapping
- records_retention_mapping

### D03 — Data, Privacy, Consent, and Information Governance

Question: What information does the AI access, capture, generate, retain, disclose, transform, or destroy?

Required evidence:

- data_flow_map
- data_source_inventory
- phi_or_pii_classification
- consent_or_notice_assessment
- retention_schedule
- deletion_or_destruction_procedure
- cross_border_transfer_assessment
- secondary_use_and_model_training_statement

### D04 — Vendor, Subprocessor, and Contract Assurance

Question: Can the vendor and its subprocessors be trusted contractually, operationally, and evidentially?

Required evidence:

- vendor_security_package
- business_associate_agreement_if_required
- subprocessor_list
- audit_rights
- incident_notification_terms
- data_return_and_exit_terms
- service_level_commitments
- support_model

### D05 — Model, Agent, Tool, and Automation Boundary

Question: What can the AI model, agent, or tool call actually do, and where are its execution boundaries?

Required evidence:

- model_or_agent_architecture_summary
- tool_call_inventory
- permitted_actions
- blocked_actions
- autonomy_level
- human_intervention_points
- system_prompt_or_policy_boundary_summary
- agent_identity_and_service_account_mapping

### D06 — Human Authority and No-Bind Governance

Question: Who is the accountable human or role, what authority do they have, and what happens if they are silent, unavailable, or unqualified?

Required evidence:

- named_or_role_based_authority_map
- qualification_requirements
- human_review_procedure
- silence_default_rule
- escalation_path
- delegation_rules
- no_bind_governance_state_model

### D07 — Evidence Capture and Audit Reconstruction

Question: Can the organization reconstruct what was captured, what the AI generated, what the human saw, what changed, who approved it, and when?

Required evidence:

- input_capture_record
- ai_output_record
- review_screen_or_review_payload
- human_edits
- approval_or_rejection_event
- timestamped_audit_trail
- evidence_hash_or_integrity_seal
- exportable_audit_package

### D08 — Validation, Testing, and Real-World Performance

Question: How was the AI system tested before use, and how is performance verified in real operating conditions?

Required evidence:

- validation_protocol
- test_dataset_description
- acceptance_criteria
- known_failure_modes
- bias_or_equity_assessment_if_relevant
- real_world_monitoring_plan
- user_acceptance_testing
- clinical_or_quality_safety_review_if_relevant

### D09 — Security, Identity, Access, and Logging

Question: Are identity, access, privilege, session control, and logging sufficient for regulated AI use?

Required evidence:

- identity_provider_integration
- role_based_access_control
- privileged_access_controls
- session_logging
- api_access_controls
- encryption_controls
- security_event_logging
- least_privilege_review

### D10 — Change Control, Drift, and Lifecycle Monitoring

Question: How are model, prompt, workflow, configuration, and vendor changes governed over time?

Required evidence:

- change_control_procedure
- model_update_notice_terms
- prompt_or_configuration_change_log
- drift_monitoring_plan
- periodic_review_cadence
- revalidation_triggers
- rollback_plan

### D11 — Incident, Deviation, CAPA, Complaint, and Safety Readiness

Question: What happens when the AI output is wrong, unsafe, misleading, incomplete, biased, or operationally harmful?

Required evidence:

- incident_response_procedure
- deviation_or_nonconformance_mapping
- capa_mapping
- complaint_handling_mapping
- safety_escalation_path
- regulatory_notification_assessment
- root_cause_reconstruction_package

### D12 — Exit, Continuity, Data Return, and Decommissioning

Question: Can the organization safely exit, pause, replace, or decommission the AI system without losing evidence or continuity?

Required evidence:

- data_return_plan
- deletion_certificate_process
- transition_plan
- business_continuity_plan
- record_retention_after_exit
- decommissioning_plan
- vendor_lock_in_risk_assessment

## Scoring model

Passport score range: 0-100

Minimum passport threshold: 80

Critical failure rule:

Failure in human authority, evidence reconstruction, data governance, security logging, or incident readiness creates ACTION NOT ADMISSIBLE WITHOUT EVIDENCE regardless of total score.

Status levels:

- NOT REVIEWED
- EVIDENCE MISSING
- CONDITIONAL REVIEW
- ASSURANCE READY
- NOT ADMISSIBLE
